      <title>New AI Visibility Insights in Bing Webmaster Tools: Intents, Topics, Citation Share, Compare</title>
      <description>&lt;p&gt;Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools introduces four expanded AI performance capabilites in preview: Intents, Topics, Citation Share, and Compare, enhancing publishers&amp;#39; understanding of AI-generated content visibility and citation dynamics. These tools build on existing AI Performance reporting to provide deeper insights into user intent, topics (thematic relevance), and citation share over time.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;ul node=&quot;[object Object]&quot;&gt;
	&lt;li node=&quot;[object Object]&quot;&gt;The Intents feature categorizes queries into query intents&amp;nbsp;like Informational or Commerce to clarify why content is cited.&lt;/li&gt;
	&lt;li node=&quot;[object Object]&quot;&gt;Topics cluster related queries into broader themes, helping identify content authority and gaps.&lt;/li&gt;
	&lt;li node=&quot;[object Object]&quot;&gt;Citation Share offers a relative measure of a publisher&amp;rsquo;s visibility compared to other sources for similar queries.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
</description>
      
      <content><![CDATA[<p aria-level="1" paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{21}" paraid="1377693286" role="heading">Earlier this year, we introduced the&nbsp;<a href="https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">AI Performance Report in Bing Webmaster Tools</a>, giving publishers and site owners new visibility into how their content appears in AI-generated answers across Microsoft Copilot, Bing, and select partner AI experiences. We&nbsp;coupled that launch with information&nbsp;about our&nbsp;broader vision behind the&nbsp;<a href="https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/march-2026/the-ai-performance-dashboard-your-view-into-where-your-brand-appears-across-the-ai-web" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">AI Performance Dashboard</a>:&nbsp;helping organizations better understand how they show up across the AI web.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{49}" paraid="1233786171">Today, we are excited to expand that foundation with four new capabilities available in preview globally within Bing Webmaster Tools: <strong>Intents, Topics, Citation Share, and Compare</strong>.</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{59}" paraid="1222822986">The original AI Performance experience helped answer an important question:&nbsp;Where is my content being cited in AI-generated answers?&nbsp;These new capabilities build on that foundation by helping publishers better understand&nbsp;why&nbsp;their content is being surfaced,&nbsp;which broader subject areas&nbsp;they are gaining visibility in,&nbsp;how their presence evolves relative to other cited sources, and&nbsp;how citation patterns change over time.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{d914b292-df9d-4cb3-bcac-952e4a9f2307}{97}" paraid="407732439">As AI-powered experiences continue to evolve, publishers increasingly need tools that go beyond traditional rankings and keyword analysis. AI-generated answers are dynamic, contextual, and often synthesized from many sources at once. Understanding visibility in these systems requires more than a single metric or surface-level citation count. With these expanded preview capabilities, Bing Webmaster Tools is expanding first-party reporting to provide deeper insight into the query context, thematic patterns, relative citation presence, and changes over time that shape how content appears in AI-powered experiences.</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{91}" paraid="1121283383">That is the goal behind these new capabilities.&nbsp;</p>
<p lang="EN-US" paraeid="{c3283ccf-85a6-4d57-8d23-d914b173c3c7}{174}" paraid="376309939"><img decoding="async" alt="Feedback_Icon_PNG.png" height="1159px" src="https://blogs.bing.com/getattachment/3c773825-c6ec-4e69-b658-787beb60d3fe/Feedback_Icon_PNG.png" title="Feedback_Icon_PNG.png" width="1623px" />&nbsp;</p>
<p aria-level="2" paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{97}" paraid="584808971" role="heading"><strong>Understanding the Intent Behind Citations&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{103}" paraid="2013944811">One of the biggest challenges publishers face today is understanding the&nbsp;context&nbsp;behind AI-generated citations. A query alone often tells only part of the story.</p>
<p>In AI-generated answers, grounding refers to the source material and web evidence the system uses to support and cite its response. For deeper understanding of grounding &ndash; refer to&nbsp;<a href="https://blogs.bing.com/search/February-2026/Elevating-the-Role-of-Grounding-on-the-AI-Web" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Elevating the Role of Grounding on the AI Web</a>&nbsp;and our&nbsp;recent&nbsp;<a href="https://blogs.bing.com/search/June-2026/Announcing-Microsoft-Web-IQ" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Microsoft WebIQ</a>&nbsp;announcement.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{113}" paraid="831644897">With the new <strong>Intents</strong> feature, grounding queries in the AI Performance Report are now classified into broader categories such as <strong>Informational, Commercial, Navigational, Learn and Solve, Research, Creation, Local, and more</strong>. This helps publishers move beyond simply seeing which queries triggered citations and begin understanding the broader query context our systems associate with those citation appearances.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{135}" paraid="156721788">For example, an e-commerce publisher may discover strong visibility in comparison-oriented or shopping-focused AI experiences, while an educational publisher may find that their content is&nbsp;frequently&nbsp;surfaced in research or learning-oriented interactions. These insights can help publishers better align content structure and depth with the types of experiences where AI systems are surfacing their content.&nbsp;</p>
<p aria-level="2" paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{151}" paraid="2081792063" role="heading"><strong>Seeing Visibility Through Topics Instead of Individual Queries&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{157}" paraid="1055131940">We are also introducing&nbsp;<strong>Topics</strong>, which group related grounding queries into broader thematic clusters.&nbsp;AI systems reason across concepts and themes rather than isolated keywords. Topics help publishers understand visibility in the same thematic structure that modern AI systems use to organize information.</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{167}" paraid="211329910">Instead of analyzing visibility one query at a time, publishers can now start understanding which larger subject areas are driving citation activity. Queries such as &ldquo;solar panels,&rdquo; &ldquo;solar energy efficiency,&rdquo; and &ldquo;residential solar installation,&rdquo; for example, may all map into a broader topic cluster like&nbsp;Solar Energy.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{177}" paraid="1563270468">This creates a more natural way to analyze AI visibility. Content teams and publishers often think in terms of themes, editorial areas, and audience interests rather than isolated keywords. Topics help bridge that gap by turning grounding query data into a more thematic view of AI engagement.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{183}" paraid="1787438282">These insights can help publishers identify emerging areas of authority, discover gaps in topical coverage, and better understand how AI systems semantically group related content.</p>
<p paraeid="{db0657df-ce0e-4a0a-ac67-5de1bc486358}{18}" paraid="1253167754">Like Intents, Topics&nbsp;are powered by&nbsp;evolving AI/ML classification systems.&nbsp;During the preview phase, some labels may still be broad&nbsp;&ndash;&nbsp;especially for highly specialized or niche domains&nbsp;&ndash;&nbsp;but the system is already beginning to reveal meaningful thematic patterns. We&nbsp;expect quality and precision to&nbsp;continue improving&nbsp;as&nbsp;the&nbsp;models mature and&nbsp;learn from broader&nbsp;real-world&nbsp;usage.&nbsp;</p>
<p aria-level="2" paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{195}" paraid="1980515329" role="heading"><strong>Introducing Citation Share&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{201}" paraid="1933783194">The next new capability we are introducing today is <strong>Citation Share</strong>.</p>
<p paraeid="{2372b491-8474-4d8b-a896-a67b28d88d49}{80}" paraid="695178859">While total citation counts show how often your content appears in AI-generated answers, Citation Share shows how much of the citation space your site&nbsp;receives for&nbsp;a specific grounding query. It is calculated as the percentage of citations attributed to your site out of all citations shown across all sites for that same grounding query. This helps publishers understand not just whether they were cited, but how much visibility they received within the full set of cited sources for that query.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{217}" paraid="1244907807">This can&nbsp;provide&nbsp;a more directional view into how visibility is evolving over time. Publishers may begin to&nbsp;identify&nbsp;areas where their content has strong and growing representation in AI-generated experiences, as well as areas where visibility may be more fragmented across many sources.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{223}" paraid="417791156">Importantly, Citation Share is designed as an observational metric &ndash; not a ranking system or a competitive scoreboard. It does not expose competitor domains, represent traffic share, or assign quality scores to content.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{229}" paraid="1909424407">AI citation ecosystems are inherently dynamic. Citation patterns can shift due to changes in user behavior, evolving models, freshness signals, partner refresh cycles, and broader changes across the web itself.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p aria-level="2" paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{235}" paraid="184392950" role="heading"><strong>Compare Changes Over Time&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{241}" paraid="1455581166">We are also introducing&nbsp;<strong>Compare</strong>, which allows publishers to overlay&nbsp;a previous&nbsp;time period&nbsp;directly onto the current reporting view.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1d590559-83cb-441c-a75f-0d760cdb8035}{251}" paraid="451284725">This makes it easier to visually understand how citation activity is changing over time and&nbsp;observe&nbsp;shifts that may correlate with content updates, seasonality, changing demand, or broader ecosystem changes.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{b5c71124-cfa5-48ff-a4b4-18506bb4bacb}{2}" paraid="1064122256">For example, publishers can compare the current 30-day period against the prior&nbsp;30 days&nbsp;or select custom date ranges to better understand evolving citation trends.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{b5c71124-cfa5-48ff-a4b4-18506bb4bacb}{8}" paraid="1165047885">Compare is designed to help publishers observe changes over time. Citation activity can be influenced by many factors including evolving AI models, competing content, freshness signals, and shifts in user demand.</p>

<p aria-level="2" paraeid="{b5c71124-cfa5-48ff-a4b4-18506bb4bacb}{14}" paraid="1364449369" role="heading"><strong>Increasing transparency&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{1cf7d995-d041-4983-9df9-ba38e135ae0d}{209}" paraid="1573375751">These new capabilities are part of Microsoft&rsquo;s ongoing effort to provide greater transparency into how content appears across AI-powered experiences. As AI answers become a larger part of how people discover information, publishers need more than raw citation counts&nbsp;&ndash;&nbsp;they need reporting that helps them understand the context, topics, relative presence, and changes over time behind those citations.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1cf7d995-d041-4983-9df9-ba38e135ae0d}{218}" paraid="703237361">That is what this work is designed to advance. Intents, Topics, Citation Share, and Compare give publishers a more practical way to observe citation activity, identify visibility patterns, and make more informed decisions about their content strategy. They do not turn AI visibility into a single ranking or score, but they do give webmasters a richer set of signals for understanding how their content is being represented across evolving AI experiences.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{1cf7d995-d041-4983-9df9-ba38e135ae0d}{227}" paraid="633463448">As the AI web continues to evolve, we will continue investing in new reporting capabilities, richer analytics, and better visibility tools for webmasters and content creators. These preview features are early steps in a broader effort to make AI visibility more understandable, actionable, and useful for the publisher ecosystem.</p>

      <title>Announcing Microsoft Web IQ</title>
      <description>&lt;p&gt;AI applications are only as good as the information they reason from. Without fresh, high-quality web data, they are less dependable. Today, Microsoft is launching Web IQ: a suite of AI-native grounding APIs built for the agentic era, connecting AI systems and agents to current, real-world intelligence from across the web &amp;mdash; including web pages, news, images, and videos.&lt;/p&gt;

      <content><![CDATA[<p>AI applications are only as good as the information they reason from. Without fresh, high-quality web data, they are less dependable. Today, Microsoft is launching <a href="https://aka.ms/WebIQ" target="_blank">Web IQ</a>: a suite of AI-native grounding APIs built for the agentic era, connecting AI systems and agents to fresh, real-world intelligence from across the web &#8211; including web pages, news, images, and videos.</p>
<p>The systems that define the agentic era will be the ones that can retrieve fresh, authoritative evidence quickly, transform it into useful context, and do so within the latency and efficiency budgets that multi-step reasoning demands.</p>
<p>Model capability alone no longer determines whether an AI system is useful. What matters is how effectively the full system connects models to the world, including information created after models were trained, and information too vast to encode in model weights.</p>
<p>Web IQ is a search engine for AI systems. Where Bing was built to help people search the web, Web IQ is built to help AI agents find the right information, turn it into useful evidence, and use it inside reasoning. Unlike other APIs that layer on top of fragile infrastructure, Web IQ is a new kind of search system, one that delivers the right evidence with the speed, quality, and efficiency modern agents require.</p>
<p>It builds on years of learning from Bing, but it required a major ground-up re-architecture to meet the demands of agentic workloads.</p>
<h2>Built on Bing, Re-Architected for the Agentic Era</h2>
<p>Web IQ starts from the foundation Microsoft has been building for decades: the Bing global index and ecosystem. Grounding quality depends on the breadth, freshness, and trustworthiness of the world representation underneath it &ndash; something that is achieved by building on Bing&rsquo;s expansive reach.</p>
<p>But the agentic era asks fundamentally different questions of the stack. Agents do not issue a single search and stop. They retrieve repeatedly, reason over evidence, adapt to new information, and operate inside tight latency budgets. Meeting those requirements could not be solved by tuning a single component. It required re-architecting the system from the ground up from indexing and retrieval to ranking, passage selection, and orchestration so every layer is aligned around the needs of inference-time grounding. That is the core idea behind Web IQ: preserve the strengths of Bing&rsquo;s foundation while redesigning the grounding stack to serve as the execution fabric for AI agents.</p>
<p><img decoding="async" alt="Shows the high-level architecture of Web IQ" src="https://blogs.bing.com/BingBlogs/media/SearchBlog/2026/WebIQ/WebIQ1.png" /></p>
<h3>At the base of this system is something that predates Web IQ by many years: the Bing global index and ecosystem.</h3>
<p>Evolving beyond a large crawl, it is a continuously refined representation of the web, built over decades through a combination of infrastructure, partnership, and discipline. It reflects millions of decisions about what to include, how to rank it, how to ensure freshness, and how to maintain trust.</p>
<p>That discipline extends to how we participate in the open web itself. Web IQ inherits Bing&rsquo;s long-standing commitment to the conventions and evolving standards of the internet ecosystem, including honoring robots exclusion protocols, publisher controls, and access preferences that govern how content can be discovered, accessed, and used. We are actively engaging with the broader ecosystem through the IETF and other industry forums to help evolve interoperable standards for the AI era. Our goal is to be a sincere and trusted participant in the open web &mdash; one that respects publisher choice and helps sustain a healthy ecosystem for content providers, advertisers, developers, and users alike.</p>
<p>The role of that foundation is often underestimated. Grounding systems cannot exceed the quality of the world they observe. If the index is incomplete, stale, or unreliable, no amount of modeling can compensate. Web IQ begins from the premise that grounding quality is anchored in the quality of the underlying corpus and that corpus must be global, fresh, honor publisher preferences by default, and continuously evolving.</p>
<h3>On top of that foundation sits the model layer, where we made a different kind of decision.</h3>
<p>Rather than building a large collection of specialized models, we focused on a <strong>small number of models that are world-class and tightly integrated into the system</strong>. These models serve distinct but coordinated roles: they analyze content, they represent it in embedding space, and they rank and select it for use inside inference.</p>
<p>One of the central components here is <a href="~/search/April-2026/Microsoft-Open-Sources-Industry-Leading-Embedding-Model" target="_blank">our best-in-class embedding model</a>, which defines how information is projected into a space where semantic similarity becomes computationally tractable. That decision alone has far-reaching consequences; the quality of embeddings determines not just recall during retrieval, but the <em>shape of the candidate space that every downstream component operates on</em>. We have built it to be competitive at the top of public benchmarks, but its role inside Web IQ is more pragmatic: when we search, we search the right neighborhood of the information space.</p>
<p>The embedding model is one part of the system. Alongside it are models that are optimized for content understanding and ranking, trained not for isolated metrics but for <strong>how their outputs are used inside LLM-driven reasoning</strong><strong>. </strong>That alignment, between model objectives and system objectives, is what allows the stack to behave coherently under load.</p>
<h3>Beneath the model layer, the problem changes character.</h3>
<p>Grounding is no longer about semantics alone. It becomes a <strong>distributed systems problem at scale</strong> and this is where a great deal of our earlier work becomes relevant, particularly with systems like <strong>DiskANN</strong>. DiskANN changed the practical limits of nearest neighbor search by making it possible to operate over large, disk-resident vector spaces without sacrificing latency, removing the need to trade recall for memory footprint.</p>
<p>In Web IQ, this work is extended into a broader retrieval fabric. Retrieval is executed across distributed partitions, routed globally, and tightly optimized to meet latency constraints. Networking, data placement, and execution paths are all part of the design space &ndash; and they matter, because grounding is not a one-time operation. It is executed repeatedly within agentic workflows and at that scale, even small inefficiencies compound.</p>
<p>What happens after retrieval is equally consequential. Web IQ does not just return documents; it returns passages and structured evidence objects. Models do not need documents, they need <em>information</em> and documents are often a poor proxy for that. By operating at the level of passages, we can concentrate useful signal while eliminating irrelevant context, producing a much higher ratio of information to tokens.</p>
<p>This is why we often summarize the system with a simple principle: <strong>fewer tokens in, better answers out, lower cost per call.</strong> Cost is only part of it. The deeper value is <strong>maintaining</strong><strong> precision in reasoning</strong> under constrained contexts.</p>
<h3>At the top of the stack sits the orchestration layer, where the system comes together.</h3>
<p>Queries are interpreted, retrieval is fanned out, results are merged, filtered, and transformed into evidence. Modalities are combined, trade-offs are enforced, and the system adapts to the structure of the request. What makes this layer different from traditional systems is that it&rsquo;s just not an outer API layer. It is part of the <strong>execution loop of an AI agent</strong>. Latency here is not just user-visible but structurally significant: determining whether a system can afford to take multiple reasoning steps or must compress everything into a single attempt. That constraint shapes everything above it.</p>
<h2>Quality, Latency, Token Density, and the Right Operating Points</h2>
<p>When grounding becomes part of an agent&rsquo;s execution loop, the core challenge is no longer retrieval in isolation. The system has to operate at the right point across latency, grounding quality, and token efficiency, because those three factors together determine whether multi-step reasoning is practical in the real world.</p>
<p>Quality is the first dimension. A grounding system also has to return evidence that actually satisfies user intent: complete, fresh, authoritative, and useful for downstream reasoning. We measure that with <strong>GDSAT</strong>, or grounding satisfaction &ndash; a metric, that unlike traditional relevance scores, captures whether the grounding truly meets user intent across completeness, freshness, and authority. Across production query sets, Web IQ consistently achieves higher grounding satisfaction than alternative systems in comparable configurations, which matters because it translates directly into greater user trust and stronger downstream outcomes.</p>
<p><img decoding="async" alt="Bar chart shows Web IQ grounding satisfaction compared to competitors" height="374px" src="https://blogs.bing.com/BingBlogs/media/SearchBlog/2026/WebIQ/WebIQ_GDSAT2.png" width="600px" /><br />
<small><em>Source: 3K Global, Blind Queries sampled from prod, Config: 10 results, 10K chars per result (or equivalent).</em></small></p>
<p>Speed is also imperative. It determines whether an agent can afford multiple retrieval-and-reasoning steps or must collapse everything into a single attempt. Web IQ is designed for production-scale speed, operating at sub-165ms p95 latency and, in our internal comparisons, nearly 2.5&times; faster than the next best alternative from the previous cohort of competitors under similar conditions.</p>
<p><img decoding="async" alt="Bar Graph shows Web IQ P95 latency compared to competitors" height="374px" src="https://blogs.bing.com/BingBlogs/media/SearchBlog/2026/WebIQ/WebIQ_P95Latency2.png" width="600px" />&nbsp;<br />
<small><em>From VMs hosted in 5 DCs: West US2, North Central US, East US2, North Europe, South Korea. P95 numbers are averaged across DCs for the cohort of competitors. Unique queries were used for avoiding cache hits. Config: 10 results, 10K chars per result.</em></small></p>
<p>The third variable is token efficiency, which determines whether the system can scale economically. Every token sent to the model carries both a cost and a latency implication. By operating on passage-level evidence and maintaining high information density per token, Web IQ reduces how much context is required to achieve a given level of quality.</p>
<p><img decoding="async" alt="Graph shows Web IQ token efficiency compared to competitors" height="728px" src="https://blogs.bing.com/BingBlogs/media/SearchBlog/2026/WebIQ/WebIQ_TokenEfficiency3.png" width="1200px" /></p>
<p><small><em>Source: 3K Query set (Global, Prod sampled). Web results: 10, 15, 20. #Chars: 3K, 5K, 10K, 20k per result (or equivalent).</em></small></p>
<p>The result is a system designed to move the frontier on all three dimensions at once: lower latency, higher grounding satisfaction, and fewer tokens per call.</p>
<h2>Architecture and Design Principles</h2>
<p>The architecture of Web IQ follows a few core principles.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>G</strong><strong>rounding is a full-stack systems problem.</strong> Trustworthy outputs depend on aligning every layer, from corpus quality and retrieval to ranking and orchestration.</li>
<li><strong>F</strong><strong>oundation matters.</strong> A global and fresh corpus is essential for trustworthy grounding, which is why the Bing foundation remains so important even as the system above it is re-architected.</li>
<li><strong>T</strong><strong>he right evidence unit is the passage</strong> or structured evidence object that maximizes information density while minimizing token cost.</li>
<li><strong>R</strong><strong>eal operating points define the system.</strong> Latency, quality, and token constraints determine whether agents work reliably in practice, and the architecture is engineered around them.</li>
</ul>
<p>The agentic web will be built by systems that can reason against the world as it actually is: fresh, contested, and constantly changing. That requires more than a model with a search tool attached. It requires a grounding layer engineered for the speed, quality, and economics of inference-time retrieval, built on a foundation the open web can trust.</p>
<p>That is what Web IQ is built to be, and where we believe the next decade of AI infrastructure is heading.</p>

      <content><![CDATA[<p>Search is changing. It is no longer just retrieval.</p>
<p>In an AI-powered environment, search engines don&rsquo;t simply index pages &mdash; more so than ever, they interpret intent, generate summaries, and surface synthesized answers. That shift fundamentally changes the user experience.</p>

<li>Contextual intervention through PSAs</li>
<li>Elevation of authoritative resources</li>
<li>User-controlled filtering via SafeSearch</li>
</ol>
<p>These AI-powered safety protections work together to reduce harmful content and connect people to trusted information at every stage of the search experience.</p>
<p><strong>Surfacing resources when users need them most</strong><br />

<p paraeid="{b501502b-6736-447d-927a-c043457fc159}{31}" paraid="77460537"><strong>What Traditional Search Indexing Optimizes For</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{b501502b-6736-447d-927a-c043457fc159}{37}" paraid="862987903">Traditional indexing answers a simple question: which pages should a user visit? The goal is recall and breadth &ndash; surface as many relevant options as possible and let the user choose. <p paraeid="{b501502b-6736-447d-927a-c043457fc159}{43}" paraid="2013946741">That simplicity is a feature. Search was designed for humans who can scan a results page, skip the results that&nbsp;don&rsquo;t&nbsp;fit, and course&ndash;correct in real time. The index does not need to be right about every result &ndash; it needs to be right enough that the user finds what they are looking for.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{b501502b-6736-447d-927a-c043457fc159}{49}" paraid="280490806"><strong>What Changes When the Goal Is Grounding AI Answers&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{b501502b-6736-447d-927a-c043457fc159}{55}" paraid="1199672102">Grounding an AI&ndash;generated answer introduces a fundamentally different constraint: the system is no longer just pointing to&nbsp;information,&nbsp;it is using it. Abstention is a valid outcome when support is missing, stale, or conflicting&nbsp;&ndash;&nbsp;it reflects a deliberate judgment about what the available evidence can justify.</p>

<p paraeid="{b501502b-6736-447d-927a-c043457fc159}{239}" paraid="1013668960">Traditional search quality is&nbsp;largely measured&nbsp;through user behavior and ranking performance. The index asks: is the most relevant content being surfaced at the top? Are users finding what they need? Is content fresh enough to be useful for ranking? Are near&ndash;duplicate pages&nbsp;being collapsed&nbsp;efficiently? These measures all assume a human in the loop who can scan, skip, and&nbsp;self&ndash;correct. A stale result is a ranking problem. A missed document is a coverage gap. Both matter &ndash; but neither is&nbsp;catastrophic, because&nbsp;the user can recover.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{b501502b-6736-447d-927a-c043457fc159}{245}" paraid="1593333957">Grounding changes what the index&nbsp;needs to&nbsp;account for, in ways that are both more demanding and harder to measure. Factual fidelity becomes critical: does the indexed representation of a page accurately preserve the meaning of the original content? The processes of breaking content into retrievable chunks and transforming it for fast lookup can distort&nbsp;page&nbsp;substance&nbsp;in ways that never appear in any ranking signal. Source&nbsp;attribution&nbsp;quality matters in an entirely new way &ndash; not all indexed content carries equal evidentiary weight for an AI answer, and the index needs to understand that distinction.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{6}" paraid="121575764">Freshness failure carries a categorically different cost. In search, stale content degrades ranking. In grounding, a&nbsp;stale&nbsp;fact produces a&nbsp;misleading&nbsp;response. The index must also account for coverage gaps in high&ndash;value content &ndash; not just whether the web is broadly indexed, but whether the specific facts and sources that people are likely to ask about are&nbsp;actually available&nbsp;and&nbsp;groundable. And when two indexed sources contradict each other, a grounding index cannot simply surface one above the other and move on. It needs to register that conflict, because an AI system that silently arbitrates between contradictory sources is one that may confidently assert the wrong thing.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{18}" paraid="751100723">The shift in what gets measured reflects a deeper shift in what the index&nbsp;is responsible for. A search&nbsp;function&nbsp;is accountable for surfacing options. A grounding&nbsp;function&nbsp;is accountable for the quality of evidence it provides to an AI system that will commit to an answer which the users may subsequently verify.</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{34}" paraid="1599797038"><strong>Grounding Builds on Search&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{40}" paraid="2114981757">A common misconception is that grounding replaces search. It does not. Grounding builds on the same foundational infrastructure &ndash; the same crawlers, the same quality signals, the same deep understanding of the web &ndash; but it adds a new optimization layer on top.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{46}" paraid="2084067006">Grounding is about&nbsp;determining&nbsp;what information can responsibly support a claim and having the discipline to withhold when the evidence is not there. The infrastructure is shared. The purpose is different.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{52}" paraid="1635579552"><strong>Retrieval Becomes a System,&nbsp;Not&nbsp;a Step&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{58}" paraid="426392294">Traditional search is typically a single interaction: query in, ranked results out. The simplicity of that model is a feature &ndash; it is fast, predictable, and easy to reason about.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{64}" paraid="714938942">Grounding&nbsp;operates&nbsp;in loops. A system grounding an AI answer may need to ask follow&ndash;up questions, refine retrieval based on intermediate results, combine evidence from multiple sources, and re&ndash;evaluate when confidence is low.&nbsp;This changes the&nbsp;error profile of the index entirely&nbsp;&ndash; in particular the retrieval systems.&nbsp;If early retrieval steps introduce subtle errors, those errors compound through&nbsp;subsequent&nbsp;reasoning steps in ways that no human reviewer would catch in real time. Grounding systems cannot rely on the safety net that search provides &ndash; where a user scans results, skips irrelevant hits, and course&ndash;corrects on the fly.&nbsp;Retrieval&nbsp;systems&nbsp;must therefore&nbsp;optimize&nbsp;not just for one&ndash;shot retrieval, but for consistent, repeatable behavior across iterative use.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{88}" paraid="1939539898"><strong>The Bigger Picture&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{94}" paraid="126332620">Indexing for grounded AI answers is not a reinvention of search &ndash; it is a major evolution of it. Grounding commits to an answer. This is not a surface&ndash;level evolution.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{100}" paraid="190278967">The shift&nbsp;we&nbsp;described at the opening is worth restating plainly: search indexing was built to help humans decide what to read. Grounding indexing is being built to help AI systems decide what to say. The infrastructure&nbsp;required&nbsp;to do those two things well is not the same &ndash; even when it starts from the same foundation.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{110}" paraid="1223622426">What makes this hard is not the technology gap &ndash; it is the measurement&nbsp;gap. We have decades of practice measuring search quality. We are still learning what it means to measure grounding quality rigorously: not just whether an answer was retrieved, but whether the evidence behind it was&nbsp;accurate, fresh, attributable, and consistent.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{f23d1af8-a9d5-4d2e-a2ff-3fc2c8e825aa}{122}" paraid="1414028882">Search&nbsp;optimizes&nbsp;for&nbsp;likelihood of relevance. Grounding must measure&nbsp;strength&nbsp;of&nbsp;evidence.&nbsp;Understanding that difference is not just an engineering concern.&nbsp;It is the starting point for building AI systems that people can actually trust.&nbsp;For a practical perspective on what this shift means for content creators, see our blog post&nbsp;from November 2025 on&nbsp;<a href="https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/resources/discover/webcasts/optimizing-content-for-inclusion-in-the-era-of-ai" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Optimizing&nbsp;content&nbsp;for inclusion&nbsp;in the era of AI</a>, which outlines concrete steps to make information easier to interpret, cite, and verify in AI experiences.&nbsp;<a href="https://www.bing.com/webmasters/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Bing Webmaster Tools</a>&nbsp;can complement that&nbsp;guidance&nbsp;helping you use real performance data to refine what you publish and how you structure it for AI-driven discovery.</p>

It is our latest open-source text embedding model series, delivering state-of-the-art performance and ranking 1&lt;sup&gt;st&lt;/sup&gt; on the multilingual MTEB-v2 benchmark.       <content><![CDATA[<p>We&rsquo;re excited to announce an industry leading open-source embeddings model built to support the agentic web.<br />
&nbsp;<br />
As AI systems evolve from answering questions to acting, grounding is the foundational capability that drives user trust for any AI agent. The ability to provide the right level of information, at the right time, at the right context. At the heart of grounding is the embedding model: the layer that does the hard work of searching, retrieving, organizing, and connecting information across diverse sources into a coherent, meaningful response.</p>
<p>It&rsquo;s for this reason, we&rsquo;re excited to announce we&rsquo;ve open-sourced our industry leading embeddings model built to support the agentic web.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Grounding quality is determined long before a model produces its final answer. In production systems, stronger embeddings translate into higher factual accuracy through better first-pass retrieval, lower latency and cost through fewer retries and smaller contexts, and more stable agent behavior across multi-step tasks.</p>
<p>In the agent era, this capability matters even more. Agents must search across diverse sources, maintain memory over time, and update context across multiple steps. In these environments, embeddings are not just a retrieval primitive. They are a foundational layer for memory, ranking, and orchestration.</p>
<p>Enter <strong>Harrier</strong>.</p>
<p>Harrier is a new embedding model series designed for the demands of modern AI systems. It is our latest open-source text embedding model series, delivering state-of-the-art performance and ranking 1<sup>st</sup> on the multilingual MTEB-v2 benchmark (as of April 6, 2026). This result reflects Microsoft&rsquo;s sustained commitment to improve grounding quality through advances in embedding models.</p>
<p><br />
(<strong><a href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/mteb/leaderboard" target="_blank">Image source</a>&nbsp;</strong>retrieved on&nbsp;April 6, 2026 &#8211; cropped for clarity)</p>
<p>Better embeddings lead to better retrieval, surfacing the right information more often and ranking it higher, which in turn can improve the final user experience: often with more accurate answers, fewer hallucinations, better citations, and stronger multilingual performance. In other words, Harrier&rsquo;s benchmark gains translate into more reliable grounding in real-world systems.</p>
<p>Harrier is designed not only to improve embedding quality in isolation, but to strengthen the full grounding pipeline that modern AI systems depend on. It supports more than 100 languages, offers a 32k context window, and produces fixed-size embeddings for each input, enabling seamless integration with vector search systems.</p>
<p><strong>Technical overview</strong><br />
We started by developing a scalable data pipeline that gathers multilingual text pairs from multiple sources and uses GPT-5 to generate a wide range of synthetic data. This process resulted in more than 2 billion weakly-supervised data examples for contrastive pre-training and over 10 million high-quality examples for fine-tuning. To ensure the highest standards, we applied thorough data filtering and rewrote the data using large language models, when necessary. After preparing the dataset, we trained our flagship model and then used it as a teacher for knowledge distillation, enhancing the performance of smaller embedding models.</p>
<p><strong>Key technical ideas</strong><br />
Building upon our prior work in text embeddings, including <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03533" target="_blank">E5</a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05672" target="_blank">Multilingual E5</a>, <a href="https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.642/" target="_blank">E5-mistral</a>, and <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09906" target="_blank">GritLM</a>, we incorporated several approaches to advance the state of text embeddings:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Large-scale contrastive pre-training and fine-tuning.</strong> By scaling the dataset size throughout both the contrastive pre-training and fine-tuning stages, we observed consistent improvements in performance.</li>
<li><strong>Synthetic data generation.</strong> Utilizing frontier models such as GPT-5, we generated multilingual text pairs at scale, employing a variety of synthesis strategies to enhance data diversity.</li>
<li><strong>Knowledge distillation.</strong> LLM-based re-rankers produced high-quality training signals and efficiently filter noisy data. Our smaller models benefit from knowledge distillation, receiving guidance from larger teacher models during training.</li>
</ol>

For deployment on low-end devices, we trained two smaller models: <strong>Harrier-OSS-v1</strong><strong>-0.6b</strong> and <strong>Harrier-OSS-v1</strong><strong>-270m</strong>.</p>

<li><strong>Promote to production</strong>&nbsp;only when the new data met or exceeded the existing quality bar.</li>

      <title>Elevating the Role of Grounding on the AI Web</title>
      <description>&lt;p paraeid=&quot;{2880e427-dd00-401c-b789-0d7cbae95434}{103}&quot; paraid=&quot;1956296376&quot;&gt;At Microsoft,&amp;nbsp;we&amp;rsquo;ve&amp;nbsp;been steadily advancing grounding technology for a while, building&amp;nbsp;on&amp;nbsp;decades of operating large‑scale search and information systems through Bing and focusing on scale, reliability, and trust. And today, Microsoft grounding&amp;nbsp;powers&amp;nbsp;nearly every&amp;nbsp;major AI assistant in the market.&amp;nbsp;While much of&amp;nbsp;our&amp;nbsp;work happens behind the scenes, it plays an essential&amp;nbsp;and profound&amp;nbsp;role in today&amp;rsquo;s AI ecosystem, providing users&amp;nbsp;with&amp;nbsp;answers that are fluent, factually correct,&amp;nbsp;and&amp;nbsp;genuinely helpful.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
</description>
      
      <content><![CDATA[<p paraeid="{2880e427-dd00-401c-b789-0d7cbae95434}{69}" paraid="1809623836">Grounding is the system that connects AI to&nbsp;current,&nbsp;authoritative information &mdash; helping ensure answers are informed by the world beyond the model&rsquo;s&nbsp;training. As AI assistants increasingly become how people interact with information, grounding is moving onto&nbsp;the critical path between human questions and digital knowledge.&nbsp;It&rsquo;s a layer that&rsquo;s often invisible, but&nbsp;absolutely&nbsp;essential&nbsp;in today&rsquo;s state-of-the-art AI.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{2880e427-dd00-401c-b789-0d7cbae95434}{103}" paraid="1956296376">At Microsoft,&nbsp;we&rsquo;ve&nbsp;been steadily advancing grounding technology for a while,&nbsp;building&nbsp;on&nbsp;decades of operating large‑scale search and information systems through Bing and focusing on scale, reliability, and trust.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{25cdd368-706b-4c6b-aeaa-ce3a3aaff9b1}{122}" paraid="1158285367"><em><strong>And&nbsp;today,&nbsp;Microsoft grounding&nbsp;powers nearly every&nbsp;major AI assistant in the market.&nbsp;</strong></em></p>
<p paraeid="{2880e427-dd00-401c-b789-0d7cbae95434}{185}" paraid="987243569">While much of&nbsp;our&nbsp;work happens behind the scenes, it plays an essential&nbsp;and profound&nbsp;role in today&rsquo;s AI ecosystem, providing users&nbsp;with&nbsp;answers that are fluent, factually correct,&nbsp;and&nbsp;genuinely helpful.&nbsp;</p>
<div aria-level="2" paraeid="{2880e427-dd00-401c-b789-0d7cbae95434}{191}" paraid="1266660383" role="heading"><strong>The&nbsp;Web&nbsp;Enters a New&nbsp;Era&nbsp;</strong></div>
<p aria-level="2" paraeid="{2880e427-dd00-401c-b789-0d7cbae95434}{191}" paraid="1266660383" role="heading">Since&nbsp;the early days of the Internet, users have been browsing with typical patterns:&nbsp;typing queries, clicking on results, and selecting&nbsp;content&nbsp;that best shapes their needs. However, as AI assistants do more of the work, it&rsquo;s the agents doing the browsing &ndash; and with&nbsp;far more&nbsp;precision&nbsp;&ndash; acting more&nbsp;as retrievers, drawn to structured, verifiable, and applicable content.&nbsp;This changes the needs of the web&rsquo;s infrastructure, and therefore the infrastructure it&rsquo;s&nbsp;built on. Enter grounding.</p>
<p paraeid="{2880e427-dd00-401c-b789-0d7cbae95434}{225}" paraid="931749279">This is driving the emergence of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO):&nbsp;the practice of understanding how content participates&nbsp;in AI-driven experiences.&nbsp;In an AI‑first world, visibility is no longer defined only by rankings or clicks. It&rsquo;s about how content contributes to answers, citations, reasoning, and&nbsp;ultimately,&nbsp;outcomes.&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{2880e427-dd00-401c-b789-0d7cbae95434}{231}" paraid="741224529">To help content owners navigate this shift, we are&nbsp;<a href="https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview" target="_blank">introducing new visibility through updates to Bing Webmaster Tools</a>.&nbsp;These capabilities&nbsp;provide&nbsp;early insight into how content contributes to AI-generated experiences, including citation and grounding signals.&nbsp;Think of this as a first window into a broader GEO toolset, one that will continue to evolve as AI becomes a more central interface to information. &nbsp;</p>
<div aria-level="2" paraeid="{7b3dad12-2a43-4138-b5c4-5a24f8056bda}{219}" paraid="1504533305" role="heading"><strong>Why&nbsp;This&nbsp;Matters Now&nbsp;</strong></div>
<p aria-level="2" paraeid="{7b3dad12-2a43-4138-b5c4-5a24f8056bda}{219}" paraid="1504533305" role="heading">This moment matters because it sets the context. Grounding is becoming a foundational layer of the AI ecosystem&nbsp;&ndash; the connective tissue between generative models and the world&rsquo;s information.  As that layer becomes more important, transparency and collaboration with the web community matter more, not less. </p>
<p>Our goal is to keep strengthening this infrastructure while giving publishers clearer insight into how their content participates in AI-driven experiences, and how they can benefit from it.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p paraeid="{0323be8d-39b4-4b3e-91f5-0966b7e1a2a9}{10}" paraid="1219116243">There is much more coming in the months ahead. Stay tuned.&nbsp;</p>

Corporate Vice President, Search &amp; AI</p>

      <title>Introducing AI Performance in Bing Webmaster Tools Public Preview</title>
      <description>Now in public preview, AI Performance in Bing Webmaster Tools shows when your site is cited in AI‑generated answers across Microsoft Copilot, AI‑generated summaries in Bing, and select partner integrations. See which URLs are referenced and how citation activity changes over time.</description>
      
      <content><![CDATA[<p>We are happy to introduce AI Performance in <a href="https://aka.ms/BWTAIpref">Bing Webmaster Tools</a>, a new set of insights that shows how publisher content appears across Microsoft Copilot, AI-generated summaries in Bing, and select partner integrations. For the first time, you can understand how often your content is cited in generative answers, with clear visibility into which URLs are referenced and how citation activity changes over time.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h3><strong>Extending Search Insights to AI Answers</strong></h3>
<p>Bing Webmaster Tools has long helped website owners understand indexing, crawl health, and search performance. AI Performance extends those insights to AI-generated answers by showing where and how content from your site is referenced as a source across AI experiences.</p>
<p>As AI becomes a more common way people discover information, visibility is not only about blue links. It is also about whether your content is cited and referenced when AI systems generate answers. This release is an early step toward Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) tooling in Bing Webmaster Tools, helping publishers understand how their content participates in AI-driven experiences.</p>
<p><img decoding="async" alt="AI-performance-dashboard.png" height="572px" src="https://blogs.bing.com/getattachment/292283cf-c4d3-45fd-9eb9-1d24ad5d67eb/AI-performance-dashboard.png" title="AI-performance-dashboard.png" width="799px" /><br />
&nbsp;</p>
<h3><strong>AI Performance Dashboard: Visibility Across AI Experiences </strong></h3>
<p>The AI Performance dashboard provides a consolidated view of when your site is cited in AI answers.</p>
<p><strong>What the dashboard measures</strong></p>
<p><strong>Total Citations</strong></p>
<p>Shows the total number of citations that are displayed as sources in AI-generated answers during the selected time frame. This highlights how often your content is referenced by AI systems, without indicating placement or presentation within a specific answer.</p>
<p><strong>Average Cited Pages</strong></p>
<p>Shows the average number of unique pages from your site that are displayed as sources in AI-generated answers per day over the selected time range. Because the data is aggregated across supported AI surfaces, average cited pages reflect overall citation patterns and does not indicate ranking, authority, or the role of any page within an individual answer.</p>
<p><strong>Grounding queries</strong></p>
<p>Shows the key phrases the AI used when retrieving content that was referenced in AI-generated answers. The data shown represents a sample of overall citation activity. We will continue to refine this metric as additional data is processed.</p>
<p><strong>Page-level citation activity</strong></p>
<p>Shows citation counts for specific URLs from your site, making it easy to see which individual pages are most often referenced across AI-generated answers during the selected date range. This reflects how often pages are cited, not page importance, ranking, or placement.</p>
<p><strong>Visibility trends over time</strong></p>
<p>The timeline shows how citation activity for your site changes over time across supported AI experiences, making it easier to spot trends at a glance.</p>
<p><strong>Important Note</strong>: Bing respects all content owner preferences expressed through robots.txt and other supported control mechanisms.</p>
<h3><strong>Using AI Performance Insights in Bing Webmaster Tools</strong></h3>
<p>By reviewing cited pages and grounding query phrases, AI Performance insights help clarify your content visibility in AI-generated answers.</p>
<p>These insights can help you:</p>
<ul>
<li>Validate which pages are already being used as references in AI answers.<br />
	&nbsp;</li>
<li>Identify content that appears frequently across AI answers.<br />
	&nbsp;</li>
<li>Spot opportunities to improve clarity, structure, or completeness on pages that are indexed but less frequently cited.</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Using These Insights to Improve Content</strong></h3>
<p>Once you understand which pages and topics are being cited, you can use those signals to guide content improvements.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Strengthen depth and expertise</strong><br />
	Pages cited for specific grounding query phrases often reflect clear subject focus and domain expertise. Deepening coverage in related areas can reinforce authority.<br />
	&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Improve structure and clarity</strong><br />
	Clear headings, tables, and FAQ sections help surface key information and make content easier for AI systems to reference accurately.<br />
	&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Support claims with evidence</strong><br />
	Examples, data, and cited sources help build trust when content is reused in AI-generated answers.<br />
	&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Keep content fresh and accurate</strong><br />
	Regular updates help ensure AI systems reference the most current version of your content.<br />
	&nbsp;</li>
<li><strong>Reduce ambiguity across formats</strong><br />
	Align text, images, and video so they consistently represent the same entities, products, or concepts.</li>
</ul>
<p>For deeper guidance on structuring content to improve inclusion in AI-generated answers, see <a href="https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/october-2025/optimizing-your-content-for-inclusion-in-ai-search-answers"><strong>Optimizing Your Content for Inclusion in AI Search Answers</strong></a>.</p>
<h3><strong>Keeping Content Fresh with IndexNow</strong></h3>
<p>Accurate and up to date content is important for inclusion and citation in AI-generated answers. <a href="https://aka.ms/indexnowAIpref">IndexNow</a> helps keep information fresh across search and AI experiences by notifying participating search engines whenever content is added, updated, or removed.</p>
<p>By enabling faster discovery of content changes, IndexNow helps ensure that AI systems reference the most current version of a page when generating answers. If you&rsquo;re not already using IndexNow, go to<strong> </strong><a href="https://www.indexnow.org/"><strong>https://www.indexnow.org</strong></a> to get started.</p>
<h3><strong>Local Business Information and AI Visibility</strong></h3>
<p>For local businesses, accurate business information is especially important when AI experiences surface answers to location-based queries.</p>
<p>In addition to using <a href="https://aka.ms/BWTAIpref">Bing Webmaster Tools</a>, businesses can register with <a href="https://aka.ms/bingplacesAIpref">Bing Places for Business</a> to help ensure that key details such as address, hours, and contact information remain current and eligible for inclusion in AI-generated responses.</p>
<h3><strong>Evolving AI Performance with the Webmaster Community</strong></h3>
<p>AI Performance in Bing Webmaster Tools marks an important step toward greater transparency between AI systems and the open web. As we expand these insights, we&rsquo;ll continue working with publishers and the webmaster community to improve inclusion, attribution, and visibility across both search results and AI experiences.</p>
<p>We look forward to partnering with you as we evolve these capabilities and continue building tools that support discovery in the next generation of search and AI experiences.</p>
